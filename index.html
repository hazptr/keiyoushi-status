    How its checked

    Each extension runs on a JVM harness, no phone. It loads popular, opens a manga and its chapter list, loads the newest chapter, then downloads and decodes two pages through the extensions own client. Anything that fails gets one retry before it counts.

    Untestable means we couldnt get a result at all, usually a Cloudflare challenge the harness cant solve. The app handles most of those, so check there.

    This runs off main, so it can be ahead of whats published. If something shows as failing here, check it in the app before opening an issue. 1.4 extensions arent covered.
